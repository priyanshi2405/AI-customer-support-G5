Brightleaf Coffee Co. - Customer FAQ

Opening hours
We are open Monday to Saturday from 7:30 AM to 8:00 PM, and Sunday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Pricing
Espresso drinks start at 180 rupees. Filter coffee costs 150 rupees. Whole bean retail bags of 250 grams are priced at 620 rupees.

Delivery
We deliver within Pimpri and Chinchwad. Standard delivery takes 30 to 45 minutes. Orders above 500 rupees have free delivery. Delivery is not available outside Pune.

Refund policy
Sealed packaged coffee can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund. Freshly brewed drinks cannot be refunded, but we will remake a drink if it was prepared incorrectly. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 working days.

Subscriptions
Monthly coffee subscriptions give 10 percent off every bag. You can pause or cancel a subscription at any time from your account settings.

Contact
For complaints or custom catering orders, email hello@brightleafcoffee.example. Our team replies within one working day.
